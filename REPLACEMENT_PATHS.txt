V45 changes:
- Break Start -> Namaz now uses two separate dropdowns: Zohar and Juma (Friday).
- Zohar is available on all selectable days.
- Juma (Friday) is visible/selectable only when the selected attendance date is Friday.
- Selecting one Namaz dropdown clears the other.
- Server also rejects Juma on non-Friday dates and rejects old Asr/Magrib break labels for new entries.
- Employee Monthly Grade card background is light teal and title is "Monthly Grade"; existing font colors are preserved and percentage digits remain black.
